## Sketchfab conversion sources

The archives in `Sketchfab/` were provided by the user through Downloads. The original model files and textures are retained alongside editable `Farmhouse_Playable.blend` and `Shotgun_Game.blend` adaptations. `Scripts/convert_sketchfab.py` creates `Export/SM_Farmhouse.fbx`, `SM_Shotgun.fbx` and `SM_ShotgunAction.fbx`; `Scripts/import_sketchfab.py` recreates these three mesh assets with their source material slot layouts and imports their base colour, normal, roughness, metallic and AO textures into Unreal. A local `Sketchfab/ImportBackup` retains the preceding UAssets. In-game texture size is capped at 2K to control memory; full-resolution source textures remain in the archives. The main ground-floor pantry is playable; the upper floor and detached side building are exterior scenery. FBX conversion reflects the Blender side axis in Unreal, so the low vent and ramp appear on the positive-Y side of the playable house, and its detached building appears on negative Y.

This selected farmhouse supersedes the earlier non-downloadable Old Farm House request. No viewer geometry or textures were extracted, and no new account was successfully created by the agent.

The new weapon smoke, flame, sparks, chips and impact materials are original procedural project materials, generated by `Scripts/build_weapon_fx.py`; they do not contain either requested Sketchfab model.
