# Knobs State Forest reference and assets

This is an original, compressed game adaptation of Knobs State Forest and WMA,
Bullitt County, Kentucky. Its layout is not a survey or a navigation map.

Reference sources consulted October 23, 2026:

- [Bullitt County Tourism](https://travelbullitt.org/listing/knobs-state-forest/):
  oak-hickory woodland, Crooked Creek, fields, three small parking areas, entrance
  kiosk, foot access, red boundary blazes and absence of lakes.
- [Kentucky Division of Forestry](https://eec.ky.gov/Natural-Resources/Forestry/ky-state-forests/Pages/Knobs-State-Forest-and-Wildlife-Management-Area.aspx):
  forest setting, Crooked Creek and Hay Ridge references.
- [KDFWR forest map](https://eec.ky.gov/Natural-Resources/Forestry/Documents/KnobsStateForestandWMA_All.pdf):
  relative creek/road/ridge relationships and blue, orange and yellow trail colors.
- [Kentucky Nature Preserves](https://eec.ky.gov/Nature-Preserves/Locations/Pages/Knobs-State-Forest.aspx):
  hardwood canopy and native grass openings.

Terrain, creek geometry, trail loops, kiosk, signs and marker placements are
original project work. The names Creek Hollow and South Woods label game routes;
their alignment and distances are designed for play. Hay Ridge and Crooked Creek
are real reference landmarks. No source photographs, map imagery, logos or
government seals are redistributed. Existing WILDLINE oak and pine meshes stand
in for the mixed oak-hickory woodland; no distinct hickory model is claimed.

Retained foliage, rocks and forest textures keep the licenses and attribution in
`ArtSource/Forest/PROVENANCE.md`. Retained dock wood keeps the attribution in
`ArtSource/Lake/PROVENANCE.md` and `ArtSource/Lobby/PROVENANCE.md`.
The menu preview is captured from the playable map.
